## A later distribution

The MVP distributes no binary of any component: the review snapshot
holds sources and pins, the release-candidate record holds digests, and
the board records hold captures. A later distribution of a guest image
(the kernel `Image` with its initramfs, which holds the init, the
management module, and BusyBox with musl; and the device trees) carries
this manifest and each notice text its rows name, and offers the
corresponding source by the pointers above, for as long as the licences
require. A distributed boot image (`be-mvp-boot.img`) also holds this
repository's monitor, under `COPYING`; distributed firmware carries its
own row's notices and source likewise.
