reading:
  Each domain hart took one CHERI fault from its guest (epc 0x802001b8,
  the test's out-of-bounds load, made in Capability Pointer Mode). After
  that, both harts faulted at the vector's first store, over and over, at
  their own trusted-stack top less 16 (tval 0x102055ff0 on hart 2). That
  is consistent with OpenSBI v1.6 handing the undelegated fault to the
  vector with the guest's Capability Pointer Mode, so that the store took
  the stack pointer, made an integer by the addi, as its authority; that
  reading is an inference. With cause 28 delegated (the firmware since
  then), the step-1 monitor with the fault guest ends in BE_MVP:FAIL
  exception instead (experiments/cheri-guest-results.txt, "cre monitor,
  fault guest").
