# Beryllium glossary

**Status date:** 2026-10-08. This scan-friendly reference consolidates
maintained project records and session research used to prepare the
orientation update. Session inventories are research input, not maintained
authority. Definitions summarize current maintained records; exact claims and
decisions remain in the linked plans, evidence, and review records.

Start with the [project orientation](project-orientation.md) for current
status and the [roadmap](roadmap.md) for the live decision queue.

## Essential disambiguations

| Do not confuse | Correct distinction |
| --- | --- |
| R8-H0 `H0` and the RISC-V H extension | `H0` is package zero of the R8 plan; the H extension is the ISA virtualization extension. H0 is non-privileged and does not validate the H extension on K3. R8 stages H1-H4 were dropped on 2026-10-08. |
| Old and current B0/B1 meanings | Before 2026-10-06, B0 / `BE-K3-L0BOOT` was gated on KVM0 and B1 / `BE-K3-VS2SMOKE` was a future two-VS smoke. On 2026-10-06 B0 was decoupled from KVM0 and the B-series redefined, superseding both meanings: B0 is now the accepted HS boot marker, and B1 the accepted R4 image, re-executing the R3 prelude, ported to K3. The private-workspace "B1 kernel variant" was a KVM0 Linux host candidate and is unrelated to either. |
| KVM0, R8, and the B-series | KVM0, parked on 2026-10-06, and R8 stages H1-H4 were dropped on 2026-10-08. Their accepted records stay as history and gate nothing. The B-series under `bringup/k3-bN/` is the K3 path of record: B0-B7 are accepted. No B-series result is a KVM0 result, an R8-H0/H1/H2 result, or retained R8 evidence. |
| Accepted, exit-ready, and authorized | Accepted means a human decision on exact content. Exit-ready means package exit conditions are met. Authorized means explicit permission to perform a bounded next action. Accepted H0 was never exit-ready, and accepting the B6 result authorized no successor. |
| Packet, target, and acceptance record | A packet supports a decision; the target is the immutable object being reviewed; the acceptance record records the later human decision. They are different commits and roles. |
| Documentation target acceptance and active-line integration | Exact documentation reproducibility target `7cf4c4a1...` is accepted on its clean local side branch, but that branch is unpushed and not integrated into the active line; acceptance does not imply cherry-pick, merge, push, publication, or runtime/proof/hardware authority. |
| Hardware available, diagnostic bring-up evidence, and hardware validation | A board can be available without any run; availability grants no authority and proves no behavior. B-series COM260 runs are diagnostic bring-up evidence for one board. No Beryllium result claims hardware validation or qualification. |
| `S1`-`S3` and translation stages or S mode | `S1`, `S2`, and `S3` are planned documentation steps: the He-to-Be synthesis, security model v1, and the MVP plan. They are not stage-1/stage-2 address translation or the RISC-V S privilege mode. |
| Current Helium and retained Beryllium pathfinder history | `../helium-te-poc` is the current Helium source of truth. `historical_he/*` and retained `pathfinder/` history are pre-fork/lost-history and exact pinned-evidence references only. |
| Proof, bounded proof, test, emulation, diagnostic bring-up, and hardware validation | A proof establishes a stated formal proposition under assumptions; a bounded proof covers a finite declared boundary; a test observes cases; QEMU is emulation; a diagnostic bring-up run records one board's output; hardware validation is retained execution against an accepted validation plan on named physical hardware. |

## Decision, status, and review vocabulary

| Term | Expansion and plain-English meaning | Where it fits and current relevance | Avoid this misunderstanding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **plan** | Reviewed description of scope, exact inputs, allowed paths, gates, claims, non-claims, and stop conditions | Plans govern bounded work when accepted | Plan acceptance is not implementation or execution authorization |
| **planning input** | Direction or analysis to be converted into an exact reviewable plan | The step 3 sequence of B7, S1, S2, and S3 is planning input; each step needs its own plan and/or acceptance | Do not call planning input an accepted plan |
| **exact target** | Full immutable Git commit and tree being reviewed or accepted | Every accepted package and plan names one | Do not substitute a branch tip, parent, packet, abbreviated hash, or follow-up |
| **candidate** | Proposed local implementation/evidence target awaiting exact review | May be clean, dirty, blocked, superseded, or local-only | Candidate does not mean accepted |
| **packet** | Decision-neutral material prepared to help a human review an exact target | Usually records inventory, validation, claims, residuals, and a requested decision | Packet is not implementation, plan text, or acceptance |
| **acceptance record** | Maintained record of a responsible-human decision on exact content | Carries the later decision when immutable target text retains pre-decision fields | It does not rewrite the target or grant unstated successor authority |
| **accepted** | Responsible human explicitly accepted one named exact boundary | B6 result `e20c55df57551244eab47e0eff59d4c71fe75d20` is accepted | Accepted does not mean exit-ready, hardware-validated, published, or successor-authorized |
| **readiness / exit-ready** | Whether all package exit conditions are satisfied | Accepted H0 was never exit-ready: blockers and `UNKNOWN` values remained when R8 was dropped | Passing some checks or accepting the target does not satisfy exit |
| **authorized** | Explicit permission to perform a named bounded action | Must identify scope, stage, and often exact base/relation; B7 implementation was authorized by its accepted plan; S1-S3 are planned, not authorized | Acceptance of a predecessor is not automatic authorization of a successor |
| **blocked** | A fact, plan, gate, check, or authority required to proceed is missing or failed | Push, tag, publication, and release are blocked; S1-S3 await their own plans or decisions | Do not weaken expectations or work around the blocker |
| **parked / dropped** | Parked: paused by responsible-human direction with its records intact. Dropped: ended by responsible-human direction, with its records kept as history | KVM0 was parked on 2026-10-06; KVM0 and R8 H1-H4 were dropped on 2026-10-08 | Dropping deletes no record and withdraws no recorded acceptance; recreating the work needs a new decision |
| **superseded** | Replaced by a later exact object for current consideration | Earlier candidates remain historical evidence | Superseded content is not silently deleted or accepted |
| **`NOT RUN`** | No retained execution/evidence exists for the named action | Hardware validation of Beryllium, B7, KVM0's K0-X/K0-R, and R8 H1-H4 are `NOT RUN` | It is neither pass nor fail, and it is not permission to run |
| **`UNDECIDED`** | No responsible-human selection has been made | Development kernel/BSP remains undecided and is not a gate for the planned sequence | Do not fill the gap with the currently installed or most convenient option |
| **`UNKNOWN`** | Required H0 fact remains unresolved in the accepted profile | Accepted H0 contains 118 `UNKNOWN` values | Unknown is not zero, absent, safe, or implicitly accepted |
| **local-only** | Exists only in local/private state and may not be backed up or shared | Many candidates and records explicitly disclose this state | Local existence does not imply remote containment, review, or acceptance |
| **root integration** | Wiring a package into root `Makefile`, `be`, and `scripts/check-be.sh` plus any specifically authorized pins | R6/R7 integration was tightly enumerated; B-series root integration is not started or authorized | It is not the same as mechanism implementation or acceptance |
| **successor** | Later exact plan or implementation related to an accepted predecessor | Each B-series acceptance started no successor; S1-S3 each need their own plan or decision | An acceptance does not create a successor automatically |
| **hardware available** | Physical equipment can be inspected or used if separately authorized | A COM260 is available | Availability is not hardware evidence or execution authority |

## RISC-V privilege and virtualization terms

| Term | Expansion and plain-English meaning | Where it fits and current relevance | Avoid this misunderstanding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **M / HS / VS / S / U** | Machine, hypervisor-supervisor, virtual-supervisor, supervisor, and user privilege contexts | The fixed profile uses OpenSBI below HS and synthetic VS subjects; ordinary S and virtual VS are distinct | Do not call VS "S mode" without the virtualization context |
| **hart** | Hardware thread, the independently executing RISC-V processor context | Accepted runtime and B-series work are single-hart; B-series board runs use hart 0. The COM260 has eight harts; the accepted MVP plan (`93967fba`) uses three under static ownership, not yet implemented | A multicore SoC does not make current claims multicore; Beryllium has never started a second hart |
| **control hart** | The hart on which the monitor does its shared work and never enters a domain: sealing, starting the domain harts, the boot barrier, console, denial log, measurement, management requests, and the time budget | Hart 0 in the accepted MVP plan (`93967fba`); not implemented | It is not "the monitor's hart": the monitor's enforcement path runs in HS on every domain hart |
| **domain hart** | A hart statically bound to exactly one domain, one G-stage root, and one VMID for a whole run, running only the monitor's HS fast path and that domain | Harts 1 and 2 in the accepted MVP plan; not implemented | A domain hart runs no scheduler and switches to no other domain |
| **H extension** | Ratified RISC-V hypervisor ISA extension | Supplies HS/VS virtualization and G-stage mechanisms; B1-B3, B5, and B6 entered VS on the COM260 as diagnostic bring-up evidence | Do not confuse it with R8 package H0 or treat QEMU or one board's runs as hardware validation |
| **G-stage** | Second-stage translation from guest-physical to supervisor-physical addresses | R2 builds roots; R3-R5 publish/switch them under a fixed scenario | It is not the guest's ordinary page-table stage |
| **Sv39x4** | RISC-V G-stage page-table format derived from Sv39 with four-root alignment | Concrete R2 backend and fixed runtime root format | R1 abstract roots are not Sv39x4 tables |
| **HGATP** | Hypervisor Guest Address Translation and Protection CSR | Selects G-stage mode, root, and VMID in privileged R3-R5 behavior | A computed value in R2 is not a CSR write or successful publication |
| **VMID** | Virtual Machine Identifier used to tag guest translations | Fixed scenarios use a bounded VMID policy, including Helium's VMID-zero discipline | Do not infer arbitrary multi-VMID scheduling support |
| **CSR** | Control and Status Register | Privileged architectural state with allowlists, masks, ordering, and readback contracts | A CSR name in a plan is not evidence that hardware behavior was measured |
| **WARL** | Write Any, Read Legal | CSR rule allowing hardware to legalize written values | One readback does not prove all values or all implementations behave alike |
| **HFENCE.GVMA / HFENCE.VVMA** | Hypervisor fences for G-stage and VS-stage translation state | Ordering around root/state changes is a security and correctness contract | Do not omit fences or generalize from one emulator trace |
| **SBI** | Supervisor Binary Interface | OpenSBI provides the bounded firmware interface below HS; fixed synthetic VS subjects have no guest-visible SBI, while B5 and B6 give their guests bounded guest SBI subsets | SBI compatibility does not select a future firmware/control-plane architecture |
| **OpenSBI** | Open-source M-mode firmware implementing SBI services | Pinned under the accepted QEMU profile; on the COM260 the BSP's OpenSBI 1.6 is trusted firmware below HS for the B-series runs | It is not part of Beryllium's HS core and is not automatically trusted without an explicit boundary |
| **DBCN** | SBI Debug Console extension | Used for bounded diagnostics from B0 on and part of the selected fixed-profile firmware interfaces | Console output is not proof of correct privilege, reset, or isolation behavior |
| **SRST** | SBI System Reset extension | Used as an allowlisted terminal reset request in bounded scenarios. On the COM260, OpenSBI 1.6 SRST shutdown halts the board without powering it off, so a human reset follows each run | A successful request is not a general lifecycle/restart implementation |

## K3, X100, and platform terms

| Term | Expansion and plain-English meaning | Where it fits and current relevance | Avoid this misunderstanding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **K3 / X100** | SpacemiT K3 SoC and X100 CPU cluster | First hardware focus. B-series RAM-only runs on one COM260 are diagnostic bring-up evidence; hardware validation is `NOT RUN` | Public documentation, board availability, or one board's runs are not hardware validation |
| **COM260** | Available K3 compute module/board platform | B0-B3 and B5-B7 ran RAM-only on one COM260; their records are under `bringup/k3-bN/results/` and in each result record | One board's runs are diagnostic bring-up evidence, not hardware validation or qualification |
| **DTB / Device Tree** | Flattened binary hardware description passed by firmware/bootloader | Future plans freeze exact identity and treat it as bounded platform input | Do not expose sensitive raw bytes or treat arbitrary DT parsing as trusted-core policy |
| **Device Tree input** | Offline normalized hardware description consumed through a bounded validation boundary | Long-term Beryllium design uses a normalized descriptor, not an unrestricted parser | It is not a runtime guest ABI or proof of hardware behavior |
| **PMP / Smepmp / ePMP** | Physical Memory Protection and enhanced locking/enforcement mechanisms | Firmware PMP handoff and locks remain uncharacterized since R8 H1 was dropped; current work does not add broad HS PMP programming | Do not infer G-stage isolation covers M-mode or firmware-owned physical protection |
| **AIA / IMSIC / APLIC** | Advanced Interrupt Architecture, per-hart message-signaled interrupt files, and wired interrupt controller | Future K3 characterization and only as much runtime use as a fixed payload needs | Presence in a datasheet is not configured, isolated, or validated behavior |
| **Sstc** | Supervisor-mode timer compare extension | B5 and B6 guests use the hardware Sstc timer on K3; B7 adds an HS timer slice through `stimecmp`, taken on K3 in its board runs | It does not imply a complete interrupt/timer subsystem |
| **state-enable** | RISC-V controls governing access to optional architectural state | A future plan must inventory and disposition accessible state before context claims; R8 H1, which would have done so, was dropped | Unmentioned optional state is not automatically inaccessible or reset |
| **IOMMU / IOPMP** | Protection and translation for DMA-capable devices and external bus masters | Deferred because current accepted runtime has no DMA; required for future device domains | CPU page tables do not contain DMA by themselves |
| **DMA** | Direct Memory Access by devices without CPU load/store mediation | Explicit non-scope for R0-R7 and the B-series through B7 | CPU isolation evidence is not DMA isolation evidence |
| **RAS / RERI** | Reliability, Availability, Serviceability and RISC-V Error Reporting Interface | Future fault-containment research | No current package validates platform-error containment |
| **PMU** | Performance Monitoring Unit | Future counter filtering and side-channel study | Counter presence is not safe guest exposure or side-channel resistance |
| **native U-Boot/NVMe path** | One-shot bootloader load from exact NVMe artifacts without persistent environment change | Former KVM0 planning direction, observed once in sanitized private-workspace discovery; history since the 2026-10-08 KVM0 drop | It is not the B-series RAM-only U-Boot `booti` path, and saved boot state must not change |
| **U-Boot `booti` (RAM-only)** | U-Boot loads an image into RAM over YMODEM `loady`, then `booti` enters it | Used by B3 and B5 and for one B6 run, under rules that forbid `saveenv`, flash writes, and boot-configuration changes | It hands off from running firmware and U-Boot; it does not characterize cold-boot state |
| **UEFI** | Unified Extensible Firmware Interface boot environment | Not evidenced and not selected for the current K3 path | Installed EFI files do not prove an active, bounded, reviewable UEFI flow |
| **kexec** | Linux mechanism for loading and starting another kernel/image without a full firmware boot | Linux `kexec_load` from the running Fedora host loaded B0-B2, four of B6's five runs, and all four of B7's runs, RAM-only | It hands off from a running Linux kernel; it does not characterize cold-boot state |
| **cold/reset-clean** | Explicitly characterized reset or power procedure plus defined pre-entry state, capture, and recovery | Required for future retained hardware claims | A power event alone does not prove architectural state is clean |

## Isolation and policy terms

| Term | Expansion and plain-English meaning | Where it fits and current relevance | Avoid this misunderstanding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ownership** | Static authority assigning a resource to one domain or the monitor | Selected baseline in Models 0.1/0.2 and the runtime direction | Ownership is not a capability parser or dynamic policy engine |
| **explicit channel** | Declared directional shared-memory or communication path | Preferred baseline for intentional sharing | Shared access must not be inferred merely from possession or adjacency |
| **capability / CAP** | Authority-bearing reference that may support delegation, attenuation, transfer, and revocation | Trigger-gated future mechanism if a concrete use case requires those semantics | Do not add capabilities by architectural preference alone |
| **TE** | Type Enforcement, mandatory label/type-based access control | Helium implements one fixed bounded TE experiment; Beryllium has not selected TE | It is not automatically SELinux, XSM, FLASK, or a production policy framework |
| **AVC** | Access Vector Cache | Dynamic policy-cache concept explicitly excluded from the fixed TE pathfinder | No AVC or policy server exists in current Beryllium |
| **CHERI / CHERIoT** | Capability Hardware Enhanced RISC Instructions and the embedded CHERIoT ecosystem | Strategic alignment and source of design/assurance lessons | K3 is not CHERI and no CHERI runtime/toolchain is selected |
| **VBS / LVBS** | Virtualization-Based Security and a lightweight VBS-like research pattern | Future sidebar/research topic | It does not authorize Windows-equivalence claims or implementation |
| **management domain** | Privileged controller side of the fixed scenario | In current accepted runtime it is synthetic HS-side logic, not Linux | Do not assume Linux services, a VMM, or a production control plane |
| **monitor** | The privileged HS-mode Beryllium software: the isolation engine that seals the configuration, owns the G-stage roots, and takes every domain exit | Also "the Beryllium core" or "the HS isolation engine"; it is the subject of invariants I1 to I9, not of the policy | It is not a domain, and it is not confined to one hart |
| **protected domain** | Synthetic VS subject with access bounded by fixed roots and fixtures; in the anchor use case and security model v1, the research domain `prot_t` whose confidentiality and integrity hold against the untrusted Linux domain | Used by R5 and Helium experiments; M2 of the accepted MVP plan runs it on its own hart, not yet implemented | It is not a production guest, service, or arbitrary workload, and it is not the monitor |
| **service domain** | Future richer VS compartment hosting non-core services | Selected broad architecture direction, not implemented | Do not treat a direction as an accepted service runtime |
| **capDL** | seL4-inspired capability distribution/declaration language | Design influence for canonical security manifests | Beryllium has no capDL parser/runtime and makes no seL4 equivalence claim |

## Assurance, testing, and tooling terms

| Term | Expansion and plain-English meaning | Where it fits and current relevance | Avoid this misunderstanding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **proof** | Machine-checked derivation of a stated formal proposition from assumptions | Models and R7 contain specific proof artifacts | "Proof exists" does not identify scope, implementation relation, compiler, firmware, or hardware |
| **bounded proof** | Proof restricted to a declared abstract model, finite configuration, selected bytes, or exact obligations | Model 0.1/0.2 and R7 use bounded proof claims | Do not shorten this to "Beryllium is formally verified" |
| **Rocq / Coq** | Rocq Prover, formerly Coq | Primary open proof ecosystem for Beryllium models and future refinement work | Current Rocq results do not prove runtime C or hardware |
| **Sail** | Language and ecosystem for executable ISA semantics | Reference for supported RISC-V behavior; mainline H-extension semantics remain incomplete | Do not claim complete H-extension validation from incomplete upstream semantics |
| **Cerberus** | Cambridge REMS executable C semantics reference | Long-term restricted-C semantics reference | Not a current fixed-TE, Model 0.2, or R0-R8 execution dependency |
| **CN** | C verification workflow associated with Cerberus/Rocq research | Research/pilot candidate | It is not a load-bearing current proof |
| **CBMC / Frama-C** | Bounded model checker and C analysis/verification framework | Research pool and possible future bounded checks | Neither proves the current runtime or hardware |
| **exact-byte proof** | R7 proof/evidence over selected linked words, relocations, and finite transitions | Accepted at exact R7 target | It is not source-level refinement, compiler correctness, or whole-image proof |
| **C refinement** | Proof that C implementation behavior refines an abstract specification | Desired future assurance layer | Explicitly not claimed for Models, R0-R7, the B-series, or Helium |
| **whole-system proof** | End-to-end proof including implementation, compiler, firmware, hardware, and environment | No such proof exists | Do not infer it from multiple bounded proofs and tests |
| **test** | Execution of selected cases with expected outcomes | Used at host, image, QEMU, documentation, and K3 board layers | Tests sample behavior; they do not prove all inputs or all executions |
| **unit / host tests** | Native tests of isolated C/model/tooling mechanisms | Strong local evidence for R1/R2/Models and package checkers | They are not target execution |
| **adversarial tests** | Deliberately malformed or single-defect fixtures/images | R6 and many rejection suites test fail-closed behavior | Finite adversarial coverage is not proof of all defects |
| **differential tests** | Comparison of canonical semantics across tools or environments | GCC/Clang comparisons in R1-R7 and the B-series; R8-H3 was dropped | Agreement can reproduce the same bug and does not establish hardware truth |
| **QEMU evidence / emulation** | Execution under the QEMU emulator with pinned images and firmware | R3-R6, the B-series rehearsals, and Helium have bounded QEMU results | QEMU is a trusted software boundary, not physical K3 validation |
| **diagnostic bring-up evidence** | Recorded markers and guest output from RAM-only B-series runs on one COM260, with output-only serial capture | The evidence class of the B0-B3 and B5-B7 board results | It is never hardware validation or qualification, and says nothing about other boards, firmware, or cold-boot state |
| **hardware evidence / validation** | Retained measurements against an accepted validation plan on an exact named board/silicon, firmware, artifacts, procedure, and capture | No hardware validation exists for Beryllium; B-series runs are diagnostic bring-up evidence for one board | Board availability, diagnostic bring-up runs, or one private host boot are not hardware validation |
| **source-to-binary** | Evidence connecting source semantics through compilation/linking to executing bytes | Future assurance milestone; R7 covers only a bounded selected-byte boundary | Do not claim broad correspondence from exact-byte extraction alone |
| **evidence correspondence vs authenticity** | Correspondence checks that artifacts agree; authenticity secures identity/provenance and trust roots | Current hashes/manifests primarily support bounded correspondence | Matching hashes do not prove the producer, run, or semantics were trustworthy |
| **trusted boundary** | Component or assumption relied upon rather than proved | Includes tools, firmware, QEMU, Git/filesystem semantics, hardware, and model cut points as explicitly recorded | "Trusted" does not mean verified or harmless |
| **non-claim** | Explicit statement of what evidence does not establish | Required in every bounded plan/evidence record | Absence of a positive claim is not hidden permission to imply it |
